Research-only registry. These are retained hypotheses / candidates, not live-trading systems or investment recommendations.

## Status labels

- **incumbent**: best current baseline worth preserving in all future comparisons.
- **candidate**: promising but needs more robustness / execution validation.
- **benchmark**: simple comparator that should remain in reports.
- **rejected-for-now**: useful negative result; keep to avoid repeating the same search.
